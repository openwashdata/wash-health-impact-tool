## 1. Email to ILF@aquaforall.org, Rick Johnston in copy

To: ILF@aquaforall.org
Cc: Rick Johnston (WHO/UNICEF JMP)
Subject: Water and sanitation-health estimation tool: an open prototype, not a proposal

Dear colleagues at Aqua for All,

Rick shared your call for a water and sanitation-health impact estimation tool. I am not submitting a proposal, and this email is not one. I am writing because the call raised a question I wanted to test, and the result may be useful to you and to whichever team you select.

The question was whether the three evidence questions in your terms of reference can be answered from the published literature in the time a consultancy has. To find out, I ran the assignment as a scratch build in one afternoon with an AI agent working under my decisions: a literature search by seed papers, authors and citations (10 577 records, 248 included works, 144 full texts read), 77 extracted effect sizes, country baselines for your eleven countries from WHO, DHS and JMP, a methodology with 24 conversion factors and their confidence ratings, an additionality guidance, and an R package with a transaction report, a small app and a flat export for Airtable or mWater.

The honest finding, in two sentences. There is strong pooled evidence for a short list: diarrhoea in children under five for point-of-use filtration, chlorination, solar disinfection and handwashing promotion, with deaths and DALYs available through WHO's attributable fractions. For several enterprise models in your portfolio (faecal sludge management services, water kiosks, safely managed on-site sanitation, institutional connections, menstrual health products) there is no pooled effect at all, so the tool reports reach and says "no estimate"; for everything in between, a confidence label and a range do the rest.

If it helps, I would be glad to be involved in the tool development phase with whichever team you select, for example by walking them through the factor tables and the governance rules, or by reviewing their evidence base against this one. Bidders are welcome to contact me as well. There is no fee attached to this offer and no expectation.
